Privacy policy

Data controller

email

What data is processed and why

  • While you take the test. Your answer to each question is kept in the server’s memory, tied to a technical session cookie, only to show you the next question and calculate your result. They are not stored in any database and are discarded when the session expires or you start over.
  • Anonymous statistics. When you finish the test, a statistical event is recorded with the percentage obtained on each axis, the language, a random identifier for the event itself and the day it was completed. It does not include your name, email, IP address or any session identifier, and it cannot be linked to you. It is used only to calculate aggregate statistics.
  • If you press “Create a share link”. The percentage obtained on each axis, a random identifier and the creation date are stored. Your name, email and IP address are not stored in the database. Anyone who has the link will be able to see that result.
  • Downloadable image. It is generated in your browser and is not sent to the server.
  • Technical logs. The hosting provider (provider) may record IP addresses in server logs for security and operational reasons. That information is not used to identify you or linked to results.

Legal basis

  • Running the test: performance of the service you request (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Anonymous statistics: legitimate interest in understanding how the service is used (Art. 6.1.f GDPR). The recorded data cannot be associated with any person.
  • Shared link: your consent when you press the button, as explained on this page and on the results screen (Art. 6.1.a GDPR and, because it concerns political opinions, Art. 9.2.a GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by asking for the link to be deleted.

Retention

Session answers are deleted when the session expires (2 hours of inactivity at most). Shared links are kept until deletion is requested. Anonymous statistical records are kept for as long as they are useful for statistics; because they cannot be linked to a person, access or erasure requests cannot be fulfilled for them.

Recipients

No data is shared with third parties, except the hosting provider acting as data processor.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of or objection to processing, and portability by writing to email. To delete a shared result, include the full link in your message. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local supervisory authority.

About the test

The statements were written to be as neutral as possible, with the same number of statements in each direction on every axis. Even so, any test simplifies reality: the result depends on how the questions are worded and which axes were chosen, it has no scientific validity, and no position is “the right one”. Use it as a starting point for reflection, not as a label.
